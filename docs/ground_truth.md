# Synthetic continuity controls

Two patches share the seam `x=1`. The left patch is `S1(u,v)=(u,v,0)`. The right patch is `S2(s,t)=(1+s,t,a s^k)`.

- `k=1`: G0 only (positions meet; tangent planes differ).
- `k=2`: G1 only (positions and tangent planes meet; normal curvature differs).
- `k=3`: G2 (positions, tangent planes, and second-order curvature meet).
